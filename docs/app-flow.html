How Secret Santa works

Every screen in order, from setting up the exchange to unwrapping your match. The organizer's steps are on the left, and each participant's steps are on the right.

Organizer Each participant
OrganizerEach participant
  1. 1
    Organizer

    Set up the exchange

    Name it, then add a budget, a date, and a note for everyone if you like. The budget and date show up on every page.

  2. 2
    Organizer

    Add people and couples

    Type names one at a time or paste a whole list. Pair up partners so they never draw each other.

    If the couples leave no possible draw, the app says so before you continue.

  3. 3
    Organizer

    Bookmark the organizer page

    This private link is your only way back. From here you copy each person's secret link and run the draw.

    Send each person their link →
  4. 4
    Participant

    Open your link

    Before the draw, the page says the elves are still busy. The same link works after the draw, so nobody needs a new one.

  5. 5
    Organizer

    Shake the hat

    Names flicker past while the draw runs. Nobody draws themselves or their partner, and confetti marks the moment it's done.

    Everyone's gift is now ready →
  6. 6
    Participant

    A gift appears

    The same link now shows a wrapped present, with a reminder to check that nobody's peeking.

  7. 7
    Participant

    Tap to unwrap

    The box rumbles, and the lid flies off.

  8. 8
    Participant

    Meet your match

    Confetti and the name of the person you're buying for. Opening the link again shows the match right away.

  9. 9
    Organizer

    Watch the progress

    See who has opened their match. The page never shows who drew whom, so the organizer can play too.

    Need a change? Reset the draw, edit people or couples, and shake the hat again. Everyone keeps the same link.
